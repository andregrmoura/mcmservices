Privacy Policy

Client inquiries, estimates, scheduling, and portal information

Effective date: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Moura Consulting & Management collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our website, request forms, estimates, scheduling tools, invoices, and client portal, including services powered by Jobber.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and service address.
  • Project details, photos, notes, requested services, and scheduling information.
  • Estimate, approval, invoice, and payment-related information.
  • Communications sent through forms, email, SMS, WhatsApp, or the client portal.
  • Technical information generated by the website or service providers, such as browser, device, or usage data.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, schedule work, coordinate projects, communicate updates, issue invoices, process payments, maintain records, improve our services, and meet legal or administrative requirements.

3. Service providers

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the business, including Jobber, payment processors, email or messaging providers, cloud storage providers, and other business software. These providers process information according to their own privacy and security terms.

4. Project-related sharing

When necessary to perform or coordinate a project, relevant information may be shared with subcontractors, licensed trades, suppliers, inspectors, consultants, property managers, HOAs, or other parties involved in the work.

5. Payments

Payment card or bank information may be processed directly by third-party payment processors. Moura Consulting & Management does not need to store complete card information when payment is handled by those providers.

6. Data retention

We retain business and project records for as long as reasonably necessary for operations, accounting, legal, insurance, warranty, dispute-resolution, and recordkeeping purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of personal information when applicable, subject to legal, accounting, insurance, contractual, and recordkeeping obligations.

9. Cookies and third-party tools

Our website and client-facing tools may use cookies or similar technologies provided by website, analytics, scheduling, payment, or client-management services. Their use may be governed by the privacy policies of those providers.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be identified by the effective date shown above.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to info@moura-consulting.com.
