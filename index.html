PROCESS
OUR PROCESS

Structured Project Management From Scope to Closeout.

A disciplined process built around planning, coordination, cost awareness, quality oversight, and project control.

01

Scope

Define objectives, project requirements, priorities, and constraints.

02

Planning

Establish responsibilities, sequence, milestones, and communication flow.

03

Scheduling

Coordinate timelines, dependencies, trade sequencing, and critical activities.

04

Budget Control

Track estimates, costs, changes, and the financial impact of project decisions.

05

Bidding & Procurement

Coordinate bids, vendor selections, contractor comparisons, and procurement needs.

06

Risk & Quality

Identify risks, monitor execution quality, and address open items throughout the work.

07

Construction Coordination

Align contractors, trades, ownership, and field conditions as the project progresses.

08

Closeout

Support inspections, punch list, documentation, and final project completion.

ABOUT MOURA
LEADERSHIP

Leadership with accountability.

Moura Consulting is led by André Moura, Founder & President, who directs project management operations and maintains direct accountability from planning through completion.

Under his leadership, we coordinate clients, general contractors, licensed trades, design professionals, and project stakeholders as one aligned project team — bringing structure, communication, and control to every phase.

André holds construction and Project Management qualifications from FIU (Florida International University), with professional credentials from NCCER, OSHA, and Procore — reinforcing the company’s commitment to safety, quality, and disciplined project control.
